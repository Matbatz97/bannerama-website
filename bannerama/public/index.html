    Artwork help

    Get your file print-ready.

    Best formats

    Print-ready PDF with fonts outlined is best. AI, EPS and SVG work too. For photos, use TIFF or high-quality JPG.

    Resolution

    At full size, 72–100 DPI is plenty for PVC and mesh banners seen from a distance. Roller banners are viewed close up, so aim for 150 DPI.

    Safe area

    Keep text and logos 5cm from the edges. Eyelets and scaffold pockets sit in that border.

    Colour

    Supply CMYK if you can. RGB files are fine, but very bright colours may print slightly softer.
